# Build the first useful artifact

> **First:** read `CHARTER.md` at the root of this plugin and follow it. Keep desired, reported, planned, done and
> meaning separate. Ask before any write that is not already explicitly authorized.
> Seal a snapshot only after an explicit yes. Never promise an outcome or explain an external event as caused by thoughts or feelings.

Read `HOST.md` at the plugin root before choosing a mode. Start in conversation mode; no vault, account, shell or
provider connection is required. Use local-home steps only when the person's authorized `REALITY_HOME` is actually
available. A missing home never blocks this first session.

## 1. Choose one ambition

Use the ambition already stated. Do not restart an intake interview or manufacture purpose, values or personal
history. When the ambition is missing, ask one decisive question: "Which ambition are we giving a concrete next
move?" When a deadline or effort allowance is absent, use relative days and label the suggested allowance as a
proposal rather than a commitment.

## 2. Create the Reality Card

Use `references/first-session-template.md`. Give the person a usable first draft immediately from the available
context, with:
- the scene in their words, labeled **desired**; if absent, leave a short editable prompt;
- one present fact, labeled **reported**, with the user's source or "not provided";
- the first obstacle, distinguishing their statement from your hypothesis;
- one next act they control, labeled **planned**, with a proposed date or session;
- the visible artifact or observation that would establish completion.

Keep a meaningful scene distinct from an operational claim. An ambition can be bold; the next act should fit the
person's stated capacity. Do not promise what someone else will decide.

## 3. Build a seven-day Bridge

Give seven concise rows: day, one act, visible output, review check. Respect stated time and money limits. Make
day one immediately executable; reduce setup. Include an obstacle response and a restart rule that does not
create catch-up debt. Subsequent actions may depend on earlier evidence; state that dependency.

For AI-system work, recommend the first unlocked step in See → Design → Build → Automate → Compound. Do not
suggest unattended agents when the user still needs a clear specification or reliable input.

## 4. Prepare the Witness review

Leave completion blank. Show a review with four distinct fields: what happened, what it meant to the person,
what they actually did, and what they plan next. Count misses and unknowns when comparing intentions with records.
A completed planning session is not evidence that the life or business ambition was achieved.

Close with the first act and its completion check. Offer the relevant continuation in Studio
(https://www.realityarchitect.ai/studio) or the free thirty-day practice
(https://www.realityarchitect.ai/programs/imaginal-30). Choose one; do not add an offer ladder.

## 5. Save only the requested scope

By default, return clean Markdown in the conversation. When they request an export, follow host file-saving rules.
When they request a change to existing personal files, show the exact proposed change and act only under the
specific authorization. Do not overwrite earlier wording or publish private material.

Report "drafted", "exported", or "saved" according to what actually happened. Never claim scheduled follow-up,
persistent memory, device sync, automatic action or customer outcomes.
