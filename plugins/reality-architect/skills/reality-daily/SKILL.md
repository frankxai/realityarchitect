# The daily practice

Two to ten minutes. No streaks, no scores. A tired day gets the one-line path.

## Morning

1. Read `soul.md` (I am, The scene, Voice) and the active aims in `reality/aims/` (or `.reality/aims/`). Read
   yesterday's log if it exists.
2. Offer the **I am** lines back to them, unchanged, in their Voice.
3. Offer **a minute with the scene**: read their scene (or the scene of the aim they choose) slowly and stop talking.
   There is nothing to force and no timer. Label it truthfully if asked: this is rehearsal and attention-setting, a
   contemplative practice; it is not a cause of outcomes by itself.
4. Ask for **one thing to look for today** (a sign, an opening, a person, a chance to act as the "I am" lines say).
   This primes attention; the evening asks whether it came.
5. Propose **one rep** from an active aim's reps, sized so it cannot be missed.
6. After a yes, write `log/<today>.md`:

   ```markdown
   # 2026-10-04
   - Looking for: …
   - Rehearsed the scene: yes
   - Focus: <aim-slug>
   - Rep planned: …
   ```

## Evening

Ask, one at a time, and accept short answers:

1. What happened today that matters? (fact)
2. Did what you looked for come? Anything else you noticed? (sign, primed or unprimed; a miss is a valid answer)
3. What did you do? Did the rep happen? (rep, move)
4. One correction for tomorrow.

Then use the `reality-witness` format for each moment worth keeping, mark the rep done if it happened, and append the
correction to today's log. Close with one sentence about what they did, not what they failed to do.

## The one-line path

If they say they have no energy: ask for one line about today, log it as a `lesson` or `gratitude`, and stop.

## Never

- Never shame a missed day or count a streak.
- Never say the day went well or badly "because of" their thoughts or feelings.
