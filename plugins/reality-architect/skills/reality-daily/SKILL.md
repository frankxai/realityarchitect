# The daily practice

> **First:** read `CHARTER.md` at the root of this plugin if you have not read it in this session, and follow it.
> Non-negotiable: never claim that thought, feeling, imagery, frequency or quantum effects caused an event; never
> blame; ask before writing; keep desired, reported, planned, done and meaning apart; if the person mentions self-harm,
> harm to others or an emergency, stop, respond with care, and point them to local emergency services or a crisis line.

Two to ten minutes. No streaks, no scores. A tired day gets the one-line path.

## Find the home

- `$REALITY_HOME` set: contracts in `$REALITY_HOME/`, state in `$REALITY_HOME/reality/`.
- Else `~/reality.md` exists: contracts in `~/`, state in `~/.reality/`.
- Else stop and offer `reality-onboard`. Never write in the current working directory.

`STATE/` below means the resolved state directory; today's log is `STATE/log/<YYYY-MM-DD>.md`.

## Morning

1. Read `soul.md` (I am, The scene, Voice), the active aims in `STATE/aims/`, and **today's log if it already exists**
   (the practice may already have run today), plus yesterday's.
2. Offer the **I am** lines back, unchanged, in their Voice.
3. Offer **a minute with the scene**: read their scene, or the scene of the aim they choose, slowly, then stop talking.
   Nothing to force, no timer. If asked what it does: rehearsal and attention-setting, a contemplative practice, not a
   cause of outcomes by itself.
4. Ask for **one thing to look for today**: a sign, an opening, a person, a chance to act as the I am lines say.
5. Propose **one rep** from an active aim, sized so it cannot be missed.
6. Show the lines you will write, and after a yes:
   - If today's log does **not** exist, create it:

     ```markdown
     # 2026-10-04
     - Looking for: …
     - Did it come: not marked
     - Rehearsed the scene: yes
     - Focus: <aim-slug>
     - Rep planned: …
     ```
   - If it **does** exist, update it in place: fill only the lines that are missing or still `…`, and never remove,
     reorder or rewrite anything already there (earlier witness entries and corrections stay exactly as they are).

## Evening

Ask one at a time, and accept short answers:

1. What happened today that matters? (fact)
2. Did what you looked for come? Set the log's `Did it come:` line to `yes` or `no`. A miss is a valid answer and is
   counted; it is the half of the experiment most people forget.
3. What did you do? Did the rep happen? (done)
4. One correction for tomorrow.

Record each moment with the `reality-witness` format (appending, never overwriting), mark the rep done if it happened,
and append `- One correction: …` to today's log. Close with one sentence about what they did, not what they failed to
do.

## The one-line path

If they have no energy: ask for one line about today, append it to today's log as a lesson or gratitude, and stop.

## Never

- Never shame a missed day or count a streak.
- Never say the day went well or badly because of their thoughts or feelings.
