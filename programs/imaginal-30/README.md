# The Imaginal Act — 30 Days

A free program from Reality Architect, the open practice for architecting a life. **Imagine it. Build it. Witness it.**

Thirty days, about ten minutes a day. Each day has one short practice and one evening entry, and ends with your own words in your own files.

## Who it is for

You want a specific life and a practice you can check. You may love contemplative practice and still want honest bookkeeping. You may keep your life in plain notes, or work with an AI agent.

## What it is not

- **No promises.** Nothing here promises health, income, relationships or any other outcome.
- **No causal claims.** Imagining, feeling or believing does not make events happen. Inner work changes what you notice and do, and what you do changes things.
- **Not therapy, and not advice.** For health, money or legal questions, talk to a qualified professional. If you are in crisis, or thinking about harming yourself or someone else, stop and contact local emergency services or a crisis line.
- **No streaks.** A missed day is information about the plan, the place or the pace, never a verdict on you.

## Two registers

Each day explains itself twice, labeled and never blended. **Meaning** is contemplative and yours. **Mechanism** is the studied pathway (attention, if-then plans, mental rehearsal, environment design, feedback) and its limits. Each day's `library` field names the entries behind it in [the Library](https://www.realityarchitect.ai/library).

## The four weeks

- **See (days 1–7).** The honest now, the Atlas, one scene, your `soul.md` lines, the first look-for. Day 7: the first weekly snapshot.
- **Bridge (days 8–14).** One aim with a verifiable done-when, the smallest rep, a dated bold move, an if-then for the obstacle, one change to your room. Day 14: a pace check and a snapshot.
- **Witness (days 15–21).** Signs primed and unprimed, openings, people and places, a lesson, gratitude. Day 21: a snapshot.
- **Compound (days 22–30).** Identity votes, a decision with a review date, the pace conversation, repairing a missed day, a system, a vow. Day 28: a snapshot. Days 29–30: the month snapshot, then keep, release, carry.

From day 5, every morning sets one look-for and every evening marks it: yes, no or not marked. Misses count with hits.

## Your files

Everything goes into the open format: `soul.md` (meaning), `reality.md` (the contract) and a `reality/` folder (`~/.reality/` in home mode) holding `atlas.md`, `aims/`, `log/`, `witness.md`, `evidence.md`, `snapshots/` and `decisions/`. Paths use `<today>` for the date (YYYY-MM-DD) and `<time>` for HH:MM. You own every line. An agent proposes and never applies edits unasked, and a snapshot is sealed only when you say it is true.

## Three ways to run it

**Reality Studio.** Open [Reality Studio](https://www.realityarchitect.ai/studio). It keeps your practice in this browser on this device; export to Markdown for a copy. Each day names its Studio view.

**The Claude Code plugin.** Install it with `/plugin marketplace add frankxai/realityarchitect`, then `/plugin install reality-architect@realityarchitect`. Run the `reality-onboard` skill once to create your files. Each day, read the day's file and ask for the `reality-loop` skill. It computes which loop is due (the day's `loop` field names the one it leans on), walks it with you, and asks before every write.

**Obsidian.** Create `soul.md`, `reality.md` and `reality/` in your vault from the [templates](https://github.com/frankxai/realityarchitect/tree/main/standard), then copy `days/` beside them. Vault mode keeps everything visible on desktop and mobile.
