# Day 16 — Openings

## Today's intent (planned)

Watch for openings today, an opportunity, a person, a door, and answer one within a day.

## The practice

1. Set today's look-for in `reality/log/<today>.md`: `- Looking for: an opening for <aim>`.
2. Vary one routine: a different route, a different seat, a conversation you would usually skip. Keep it small and safe.
3. Stay relaxed. Openings are easier to see when you are not straining for them.
4. When you notice one, note it as it happens. A phone note is fine.
5. Before tomorrow evening, do one thing with it: reply, ask, or put a time in your calendar. An opening you do not answer stays a possibility.

## Why it works

**Meaning.** Openings can feel like small gifts inside an ordinary day. Noticing them may feel like a kind of attentiveness to your own life. Whatever an opening means to you, you give that meaning a shape when you answer it.

**Mechanism.** In small studies, people who saw themselves as lucky were more relaxed and noticed opportunities others missed. Part of luck is attention, and part is varying routines so you meet more chances. These are small studies: a useful lens, not settled science. An opening matters through what you do with it, which is why the Did line counts.

## Tonight (reported)

Add an entry at the top of `reality/witness.md`, headed `### <today> <time> · opening`. If it belongs to your aim, end it with `- Bridge: <slug> · Domain: <domain>` (Studio: Witness).

- **Happened (fact):** The opening, as an observer would describe it.
- **Meant (my meaning):** What it means to you.
- **Did (action):** What you already did with it.
- **Next (planned):** What you will do, and by when.
- **Did it come?** In the log: `- Did it come: yes`, `no` or `not marked`. No opening today is a counted no, and an ordinary day.
