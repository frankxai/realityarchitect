# Day 19 — Gratitude

## Today's intent (planned)

Write down three things that went well and why, and keep one of them in `soul.md`.

## The practice

1. Set today's look-for in `reality/log/<today>.md`.
2. Tonight, write three things that went well today or this week. Small counts: a good coffee, a kind message, a rep done on a tired day.
3. For each, write why it went well. The why often names your part, or someone else's.
4. Log each one as its own entry (see Tonight).
5. Choose one to keep for hard days, and add it under `## Gratitude` in `soul.md` (Studio: Soul).

## Why it works

**Meaning.** Gratitude notices what is already good without waiting for the scene to arrive. It can sit beside wanting more; they are not rivals. What you are grateful for is a quiet map of what you love, and it is yours.

**Mechanism.** Controlled studies find that writing down what went well, and why, a few times a week can raise wellbeing. The effects are modest and consistent. Gratitude is not a lever on events, and it is not a treatment for depression. If your low days are heavy or long, a doctor or therapist is the right next step.

## Tonight (reported)

Three entries at the top of `reality/witness.md`, each headed `### <today> <time> · gratitude` (Studio: Witness):

- **Happened (fact):** What went well.
- **Meant (my meaning):** Why it mattered to you.
- **Did (action):** Anything you already did because of it, such as a thank-you.
- **Next (planned):** Optional.
- **Did it come?** In the log: `- Did it come: yes`, `no` or `not marked`.
