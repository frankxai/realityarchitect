# Day 24 — The pace conversation

## Today's intent (planned)

Read the last 14 days of your aim honestly and choose a plan you can keep, with release on the table.

## The practice

1. Set today's look-for in `reality/log/<today>.md`.
2. Count the last 14 days: planned reps (your reps per week, times two) and `rep` entries with `Bridge: <slug>`; bold moves done, and any past their date. On pace means at least 80 percent of planned reps and nothing overdue. Behind means at least half, with at most one overdue move. Below that: not enough for the date.
3. Ask, one at a time: would a smaller rep help? An environment change? A person to do it with? A new date?
4. If the date has passed or every move is done, ask: achieved, extend, or release? Release is information, not defeat.
5. Change one thing only. Write it in `reality/aims/<slug>.md` (Studio: Bridges), with a `## Is it enough? (computed <today>)` heading and the numbers under it. With the plugin, `reality-loop` walks `pace` and proposes the edit before writing it.

## Why it works

**Meaning.** This is a conversation, not a trial. You can ask your plan what it needs, the way you would ask a tired friend. Some aims deserve more of you; some need a different shape; some have finished teaching you. Choosing among them is part of the practice.

**Mechanism.** When reps slip, size, cue and friction are the parts you can change, and changing one at a time shows which mattered. If-then plans and stable cues help you start and stay on track. They do not create motivation for an aim you no longer hold, which is why release stays on the table. The numbers describe the plan, never you.

## Tonight (reported)

Add an entry at the top of `reality/witness.md`, headed `### <today> <time> · lesson` or `· rep`, and ending with `- Bridge: <slug> · Domain: <domain>` (Studio: Witness):

- **Happened (fact):** The numbers, and the one change you made.
- **Meant (my meaning):** How the conversation with your plan felt.
- **Did (action):** Only what is done.
- **Next (planned):** The next rep under the new plan.
- **Did it come?** In the log: `- Did it come: yes`, `no` or `not marked`.
