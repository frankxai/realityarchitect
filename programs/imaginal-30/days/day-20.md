# Day 20 — From sign to act

## Today's intent (planned)

Take one sign or opening from your ledger, honor what it meant to you, and do one thing because of it.

## The practice

1. Set today's look-for in `reality/log/<today>.md`.
2. Read this week's entries in `reality/witness.md`. Find one sign or opening with an empty `Did (action)` line.
3. Read its `Meant` line again. Ask: if I take this seriously, what small act follows? Send, ask, start, book.
4. Do it today, or give it a date and put it in your calendar.
5. Leave the old entry as it is. Tonight's new entry points back to it.

## Why it works

**Meaning.** A sign's meaning is yours. Nobody can tell you what a coincidence says to you. Taking it seriously can mean answering it with your hands. Whether you read the moment as guidance, as a mirror or as a reminder, the act is your reply.

**Mechanism.** A meaningful coincidence can work as a prompt: a cue that brings an aim to mind and makes a next act easier to choose. That is its pathway, through attention to action. The ledger keeps the sign, its meaning and the act on separate lines, so the record never credits a sign with an act you took.

## Tonight (reported)

Add an entry at the top of `reality/witness.md` (Studio: Witness). Head it `### <today> <time> · move` if the act took courage, `· rep` if it was part of your practice, or `· win` if it voted for an "I am" line. For a move or a win, also add a line to `reality/evidence.md`.

- **Happened (fact):** The act, and the date of the sign it answered.
- **Meant (my meaning):** What answering it meant to you.
- **Did (action):** Only what is done.
- **Next (planned):** If you set a date instead of acting, write it here.
- **Did it come?** In the log: `- Did it come: yes`, `no` or `not marked`.
