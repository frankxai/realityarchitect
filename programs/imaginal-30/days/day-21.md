# Day 21 — Three weeks, counted

## Today's intent (planned)

Seal your third weekly snapshot, with an honest tally of every look-for since day 5.

## The practice

1. Set today's look-for in `reality/log/<today>.md`.
2. Count the week since day 14: entries by kind, signs primed and unprimed, look-fors set, came and missed, and pace for your aim as on day 14.
3. Then count every look-for since day 5: set, came, missed, not marked. Which kinds of look-for came often? Which never came?
4. Answer the four questions: true now, what changed, grateful for, one correction.
5. If it is true for you, seal it in `reality/snapshots/<today>.md` (Studio: Timeline) with `cadence: weekly` and `period: <day 15> → <today>`, in the same shape as day 7. Add the look-for totals since day 5 under `## Reflection`. With the plugin, `reality-loop` walks `weekly` and seals only after your yes.

## Why it works

**Meaning.** These weeks of honest notes are a portrait of what you pay attention to. Look at it with curiosity. If your signs gather around a theme, that may mean something to you. You are free to name it in the reflection, as your meaning.

**Mechanism.** The tally records your attention, not the world's replies. Look-fors that came often are things your days already hold, or things you have learned to see. Look-fors that never came tell you how specific or likely they were. A tally of hits alone would prove nothing, because it could not have come out any other way. Expectation shapes what you perceive; that is the layer this measures.
