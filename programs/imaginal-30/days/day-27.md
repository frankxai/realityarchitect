# Day 27 — A small vow

## Today's intent (planned)

Write one vow you keep regardless of mood, with a tired-day version small enough for a very hard day.

## The practice

1. Set today's look-for in `reality/log/<today>.md`.
2. Ask: of everything in this practice, which one thing do I want to keep for years? The evening line, the rep, the look-for, the weekly seal?
3. Write it as a vow: "I write one honest line every evening."
4. Write its tired-day version, for days when you are sick, grieving or overwhelmed: "On a hard day, one sentence counts."
5. Add both under `## Vows` in `soul.md` (Studio: Soul). Few and kept beats many and broken.
6. Optional: add the tired-day version under `## State` in `reality.md`, as a non-negotiable your agents should protect.

## Why it works

**Meaning.** A vow is a thread through good days and bad ones. It is not a test of worth. It is a choice about who you are being, renewed each time you keep it. Character can be tended like a garden, slowly. A small vow kept for years can mean more than a grand vow kept briefly.

**Mechanism.** Habits form through repetition in a stable context over weeks to months, so the version you can keep every day matters more than the one you keep on good days. A tired-day minimum keeps the cue and the routine alive when the full rep is out of reach. This is the One Law at small scale: attention and belief become results only through action, repeated.
