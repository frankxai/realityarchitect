# Day 29 — The month snapshot

## Today's intent (planned)

Re-score the Atlas, compare it with your first week, and seal your first monthly snapshot.

## The practice

1. Set today's look-for in `reality/log/<today>.md`.
2. Without looking back, score the twelve domains again in `reality/atlas.md` (Studio: Atlas): now and wanted, 0 to 10. A blank stays a blank. Change the heading to `# Atlas — updated <today>`.
3. Open the Atlas in your day 7 snapshot. For each domain that moved, ask: can I point to a fact line in my ledger, or did my view of it change? Both are real, and they are different.
4. List the month: aims closed or changed, decisions due, entries by kind, signs primed and unprimed, look-fors set, came and missed.
5. Answer the four questions: true now, what changed, grateful for, one correction.
6. If it is true for you, seal `reality/snapshots/<today>.md` (Studio: Timeline) with `cadence: monthly` and `period: <day 1> → <today>`. With the plugin, `reality-loop` walks `monthly` and writes only the scores you give.

## Why it works

**Meaning.** A month is a chapter. You can read it as its author: honest about what happened, free about what it means. If a score rose, let yourself notice. If a score fell, let it be part of the story without becoming the whole of it.

**Mechanism.** Your scores are perceptions, built from expectation and evidence. A score can move because something in your life changed, or because what you notice changed. Tying each move to a fact line shows which. Comparing with a sealed record, not memory, keeps the comparison fair. No change in a score shows that a thought acted on events.
