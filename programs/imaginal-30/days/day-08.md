# Day 8 — One aim, one done-when

## Today's intent (planned)

Turn one priority domain into one aim, with a result someone else could check and a date.

## The practice

1. Set today's look-for in `reality/log/<today>.md`.
2. From your priority domains, choose one aim for the coming weeks. One. The others wait.
3. Write a done-when that is verifiable: "ten pages sent to my editor," not "progress on the book." Could a friend check it without asking how you feel?
4. Choose a date, far enough to be possible and near enough to matter. If you are unsure, treat it as a guess you will review on day 14.
5. Create `reality/aims/<slug>.md`, where the slug is a short name such as `ten-pages` (Studio: Bridges). Front matter: `aim: Send ten pages to my editor`, `domain: craft`, `by: <date>`, `status: active`. Then the title `# Send ten pages to my editor`, the line `Done when (verifiable): ten pages sent to my editor.`, a `## The scene (desired)` heading with your scene, and a `## True now (reported)` heading with one sentence.
6. The domain is one short id: body, mind, heart, character, spirit, love, lineage, circle, wealth, craft, sanctuary or golden-age. Optional: add the aim as one line under `## Aims` in `reality.md`, which is yours to edit.

## Why it works

**Meaning.** An aim is a promise to yourself at a size you can keep. Choosing it also means setting others down for now, which can feel like a loss. Let it be a choice made with care, not a verdict on what matters in your life.

**Mechanism.** Specific, difficult goals outperform vague ones across a large body of research. A verifiable done-when lets you tell later whether the aim was met without arguing with yourself, and a date makes pace computable. None of this promises the result. It makes the work visible, so you can adjust the plan instead of guessing.

## Tonight (reported)

Add an entry at the top of `reality/witness.md` with the truest kind: often `win` (you chose) or `lesson` (choosing was hard). Head it `### <today> <time> · win` or `· lesson`, and end it with `- Bridge: <slug> · Domain: <domain>` (Studio: Witness).

- **Happened (fact):** The aim and its date, in one line.
- **Meant (my meaning):** What it means to you to choose this one.
- **Did (action):** Only what is done.
- **Next (planned):** Tomorrow: the smallest rep.
- **Did it come?** In the log: `- Did it come: yes`, `no` or `not marked`.
