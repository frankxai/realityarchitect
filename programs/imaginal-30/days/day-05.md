# Day 5 — The first look-for

## Today's intent (planned)

Choose one thing to look for today, write it down before the day starts, and mark tonight whether it came. Hits and misses both count.

## The practice

1. Read your scene and your "I am" lines. One minute.
2. Choose one look-for that a day could plausibly bring and that you would recognize: "a conversation about the book," "a moment I choose the chapter over my phone," "an offer of help." Make it specific enough that tonight the answer could be no.
3. Write it first, before the day starts, in a new file `reality/log/<today>.md` (Studio: Today): the heading `# <today>`, then `- Looking for: a conversation about the book` and `- Rehearsed the scene: yes`.
4. Go about your day. No straining, no hunting. From today on, every morning starts with this line.

## Why it works

**Meaning.** A look-for can feel like a small conversation with your own life: a question asked in the morning and listened for all day. Whether what comes feels like a sign is yours to say. Keep the question light. The asking is the practice.

**Mechanism.** Setting an intention primes attention, so related things get noticed more often once you are looking. That is why look-fors seem to come true so often, and why they need honest bookkeeping. Writing the prediction first and recording misses with hits turns it into a fair test of your attention, never of your worth. One day shows nothing either way; read the tally over weeks.

## Tonight (reported)

In `reality/log/<today>.md`, add `- Did it come: yes`, `no` or `not marked`. A no is a counted miss, not a failure.

If it came, add an entry at the top of `reality/witness.md`, headed `### <today> <time> · sign · primed`, because you had set out to notice it. Something meaningful you were not looking for gets `· sign · unprimed` (Studio: Witness).

- **Happened (fact):** What an outside observer could have seen.
- **Meant (my meaning):** What it meant to you, in your words.
- **Did (action):** What you already did in response, if anything.
- **Next (planned):** What you intend to do.
- **Did it come?** Already marked above: yes, no, or not marked.
