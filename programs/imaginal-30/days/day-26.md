# Day 26 — Let a system carry it

## Today's intent (planned)

Choose one step you have carried by willpower and hand it to a system: a default, a reminder, a checklist or an agent.

## The practice

1. Set today's look-for in `reality/log/<today>.md`.
2. Look back over three weeks. Which step slipped again and again? The look-for, the evening entry, the rep, the weekly seal?
3. Choose one system for it. A recurring calendar event with the day log's path in it. A checklist on the wall. A note on your phone's lock screen. With the plugin, a daily reminder in your own calendar that says "run my loop" starts the `reality-loop` skill; the plugin never schedules anything itself.
4. Decide how you will know it works: "the evening entry happens on 5 of 7 nights."
5. Write it under `## Systems` in `reality.md`, which is yours to edit, and in `reality/systems.md` with a review date. If it serves your aim, add it under `- Systems to build:` in `reality/aims/<slug>.md` (Studio: Bridges).

## Why it works

**Meaning.** A system is a promise kept on your behalf on the days you cannot keep it yourself. Building it is a kindness to your future tired self. It frees your attention for the parts of the practice that need you: the scene, the meaning, the choice.

**Mechanism.** Willpower varies with sleep, stress and mood; defaults, cues and reminders do not. A system that removes one decision makes the action likelier to happen in a stable context, which is how habits form. Give it a measure and a review date, so you can tell whether it helps instead of assuming it does.
