# Day 11 — The obstacle and the if-then

## Today's intent (planned)

Name the obstacle inside you that tends to stop the rep, and decide now what you will do when it shows up.

## The practice

1. Set today's look-for in `reality/log/<today>.md`.
2. Read your scene. Then ask: what in me tends to get in the way? Not the economy, not other people: the habit, the feeling, the moment. "I open messages first and the morning is gone."
3. Name its gap class: knowledge, capability, resource, coordination, technology, permission, process, evidence or time.
4. Write an if-then: "If I reach for my phone before the paragraph, then I put it in the drawer and write one sentence."
5. In `reality/aims/<slug>.md`, add a `## Obstacle and plan (planned)` heading with two lines: `- Obstacle: I open messages first · Gap class: process` and `- If I reach for my phone before the paragraph, then I put it in the drawer and write one sentence.` (Studio: Bridges).
6. Picture the moment once, and see yourself doing the then.

## Why it works

**Meaning.** Naming the obstacle is not pessimism. It lets the scene meet your real life, with its habits and tired evenings, and you choose it anyway. There is a quiet dignity in planning for your own weak moment instead of pretending it will not come.

**Mechanism.** In experiments, picturing the wanted outcome and then the obstacle, combined with an if-then plan, improved follow-through compared with positive fantasy alone. A meta-analysis of 94 tests found a medium-to-large effect of if-then plans on reaching goals, over and above the goal itself. They help you start and stay on track. They do not create motivation for a goal you do not hold.

## Tonight (reported)

Did the obstacle show up? If it did and you used the then, add an entry headed `### <today> <time> · win`. If it did and you did not, `· lesson`. If it stayed away, log the day's rep as `· rep`. Put it at the top of `reality/witness.md`, ending with `- Bridge: <slug> · Domain: <domain>` (Studio: Witness).

- **Happened (fact):** What happened at the moment of the obstacle.
- **Meant (my meaning):** What it showed you about yourself or the plan.
- **Did (action):** Only what is done.
- **Next (planned):** Keep the if-then, or one change to it.
- **Did it come?** In the log: `- Did it come: yes`, `no` or `not marked`.
