# Day 18 — A lesson day

## Today's intent (planned)

Take one thing that did not go as planned and turn it into one correction, without a verdict on yourself.

## The practice

1. Set today's look-for in `reality/log/<today>.md`.
2. Find one miss from the last week: a no on a look-for, a skipped rep, a move past its date, a moment you would replay.
3. Write it as an observer would. One sentence, no adjectives about yourself.
4. Ask three questions. The plan: too big, wrong time? The environment: a missing cue, too much friction? The pace: too much at once? Note what was outside your control, too.
5. Write one correction you can try tomorrow, as an if-then if it fits.
6. Put it in the log as `- One correction: …` (Studio: Today).

## Why it works

**Meaning.** A miss can be a teacher that asks only for attention. You can let it be information instead of a judgment of who you are. There is freedom in saying plainly: this did not work, here is what I will try. Then you let the day close.

**Mechanism.** Separating what is up to you from what is not, then reviewing the day, is an old practice behind modern reappraisal and after-action reviews. Locating the cause in the plan, the environment or the pace points at something you can change; a verdict on your character points nowhere. One correction at a time shows which change helped.

## Tonight (reported)

Add an entry at the top of `reality/witness.md`, headed `### <today> <time> · lesson` (Studio: Witness):

- **Happened (fact):** The miss, in one observer's sentence.
- **Meant (my meaning):** What it taught you.
- **Did (action):** Only what is done.
- **Next (planned):** The correction, and when you will try it.
- **Did it come?** In the log: `- Did it come: yes`, `no` or `not marked`.
