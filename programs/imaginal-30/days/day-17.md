# Day 17 — People and places

## Today's intent (planned)

Name the people and places that belong on your bridge, and send one invitation the other person is free to decline.

## The practice

1. Set today's look-for in `reality/log/<today>.md`.
2. Ask: who has done something like this aim, or could do it with me? Which place would make the rep easier: a library, a class, a room where people already work this way?
3. Pick one person and one place.
4. Write the person a short, honest message: what you are working on, one clear ask, an easy way to say no. Send it, or set a time to.
5. Under `## Bridge` in `reality/aims/<slug>.md`, add `- People and places:` and, under it, indented two spaces, `- person: a friend who finished a novel — ask how she kept going (wish)` and `- place: the library reading room on Saturdays (wish)` (Studio: Bridges). Change `wish` to `reached` once you have actually made contact.

## Why it works

**Meaning.** Nobody builds a life alone. Reaching out is an act of trust, and asking well is a gift to the other person too: it leaves them free. Your part is the ask. Their answer belongs to them, and either answer can be received with grace.

**Mechanism.** Watching people like you succeed is a studied source of belief in your own ability, and the rooms and peers around you shape behavior more than willpower does. Doing a rep with someone adds a cue and a reason to show up. A no is information about fit or timing. Plan your own ask; never plan to steer someone else's choice.

## Tonight (reported)

If you sent the message, add an entry at the top of `reality/witness.md` headed `### <today> <time> · move`, and one line to `reality/evidence.md`. Otherwise use `· rep` or `· lesson`. End it with `- Bridge: <slug> · Domain: <domain>` (Studio: Witness). Write only what the other person chose to share with you.

- **Happened (fact):** What you sent, and any reply.
- **Meant (my meaning):** What asking meant to you.
- **Did (action):** Only what is done.
- **Next (planned):** Your next step with this person or place.
- **Did it come?** In the log: `- Did it come: yes`, `no` or `not marked`.
