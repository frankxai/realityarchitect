# Day 4 — Lines for soul.md

## Today's intent (planned)

Write the first lines of your inner contract: what you value, who you are being, and how you want to be spoken to.

## The practice

1. Under `## Values` in `soul.md`, write the five values you rank highest, in order. When two collide, the higher one wins.
2. Under `## I am`, write two or three lines in the present tense, each something you can act on today: "I am someone who finishes what I start." Not "I am rich," and never a line about what another person feels or does. Write a line you can vote for with your own hands.
3. Under `## Voice`, choose `gentle`, `direct` or `challenging`, and add one thing an agent must never say to you.
4. Read the lines aloud once. Change any word that sounds like someone else's.
5. Save `soul.md` (Studio: Soul). Agents may propose edits to it; only you apply them.

## Why it works

**Meaning.** These lines are yours, in your own language. Some people read them as a promise to themselves; some as a reminder of who they already are on a good day. Whatever they mean to you, they give you a place to stand when the day pushes. Kind, specific words belong here.

**Mechanism.** An "I am" line works through behavior. Each act that matches it is a small vote for that identity, and acting from a chosen identity makes the matching choice easier. Reflecting on your values can lower defensiveness when things go badly. These effects are real and modest. A line you cannot act on today gives the votes nothing to land on.
