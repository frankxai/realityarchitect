# Day 30 — Keep, release, carry

## Today's intent (planned)

Decide what to keep, what to release and what to carry into the next 30 days, and write it where you will see it tomorrow.

## The practice

1. Set today's look-for in `reality/log/<today>.md`.
2. For your aim, choose one: achieved (the done-when is true), extend (a new `by:` date and one change from the pace check), or release. In `reality/aims/<slug>.md`, set `status:` to `achieved`, `active` or `released` (Studio: Bridges). Release is information, not defeat.
3. For each practice from this month (the look-for, the scene, the rep, the evening entry, the weekly seal, the decision record), choose keep or release. Keep three at most.
4. Read your scene. Keep it, or rewrite it under `## The scene` in `soul.md` with what you now know.
5. Write three lines in today's log: `- Keep: …` for the practices that stay, `- Release: …` for what you set down, and `- Carry: …` for unfinished work you bring forward, such as an extended aim or a correction.
6. Put the next weekly seal in your own calendar, seven days from today.

## Why it works

**Meaning.** Endings are also thresholds. What you release is not lost; it made room. What you keep has shown it can live in ordinary days. Whatever this month meant to you, you can say it in your own words and let what comes next begin there.

**Mechanism.** Thirty days does not form a habit. In a field study, habits took from 18 to 254 days to form, about 66 on average, so keeping a few practices at a size that survives hard days gives repetition the months it needs. Releasing an aim you no longer hold is sound planning: plans help you act on a goal, but they cannot supply the wish to pursue it.
