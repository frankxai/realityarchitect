# Day 9 — The smallest rep

## Today's intent (planned)

Choose a rep small enough to do on a bad day, anchor it to something you already do, and do it once today.

## The practice

1. Set today's look-for in `reality/log/<today>.md`, and add `- Focus: <slug>` for your aim.
2. Name the repeated action that moves your aim. Shrink it until it takes two to ten minutes: one paragraph, ten push-ups, one call.
3. Anchor it to something you already do every day: "After I pour my morning coffee, I write one paragraph."
4. Decide how many times per week. Three is plenty to start.
5. In `reality/aims/<slug>.md`, add a `## Bridge` heading with the line `- Reps:` and, under it, indented two spaces, `- 3× per week — one paragraph after coffee` (Studio: Bridges).
6. Do it once today.

## Why it works

**Meaning.** A small rep is a way of keeping faith with the scene on an ordinary day. It asks nothing heroic. Each time you do it, you may let it count as a quiet yes to the person in your "I am" lines. That meaning is yours to give it.

**Mechanism.** Repetition in a stable context makes an action more automatic over weeks to months, and an existing routine supplies the context. Belief in your own ability grows mainly from small, real successes, so a rep sized to succeed builds more than a heroic one you skip. Size is a setting, not a judgment. You can raise it later.

## Tonight (reported)

If you did the rep, add an entry at the top of `reality/witness.md`, headed `### <today> <time> · rep` and ending with `- Bridge: <slug> · Domain: <domain>` (Studio: Witness). Reps logged this way are what the pace check counts. If you did not, a `lesson` entry is just as useful.

- **Happened (fact):** What you did, when and where.
- **Meant (my meaning):** What it was like, in your words.
- **Did (action):** Only what is done.
- **Next (planned):** The next rep, with its anchor.
- **Did it come?** In the log: `- Did it come: yes`, `no` or `not marked`.
