## 4. Audio programs, music and the hub

### 4.1 Audio

- **Model and voice.**
  - Use ElevenLabs Multilingual v2 or v3; avoid any model marked beta, because beta output is excluded from the
    commercial terms.
  - Professional Voice Cloning is allowed only for your own voice, so Frank's clone is permitted and nobody else's.
- **Production.**
  - Synthesize each text segment separately.
  - Insert real silences of the marked length rather than SSML breaks.
  - Duck the music bed under the voice.
  - Normalize to about −16 LUFS integrated, with a −1 dBTP ceiling.
  - Export MP3 at 192 kbps with chapter titles, plus one M4B per program for audiobook players.
- **Disclosure.** Every track opens by saying the voice is synthesized. The edition page says so too.
- **Personal tracks (week 4–6).**
  - Cost estimate: a 10-minute track is about 7,000 characters, which costs $0.56 at the $0.08/1k API price. That fits
    inside Architect.
  - Privacy: the person's words go to the voice provider only on an explicit "make my track" action, and we say which
    provider and why.

### 4.2 Music

- **What exists.** Frank's catalog (`frankx.ai-vercel-website/data/music/catalog.csv`) has 121 tracks made with Suno v5
  and released through DistroKid.
- **Two problems with it.**
  - Only about seven are calm instrumentals, and most of those are titled and prompted around "528 Hz healing
    frequency". Reality Architect cannot carry a frequency-healing claim, even in a credit line.
  - The tracks run about three minutes, which is short for a ten-minute rehearsal.
- **Recommendation: make five new instrumental beds named after the cues** (`arrive`, `rehearse`, `rise`, `witness`,
  `seal`), each 6–10 minutes.
  - Make them in Frank's own Suno workflow, on Pro or Premier, because rights follow the plan at creation.
  - Do not register them for Content ID, so buyers never get claims.
  - "Magical Times" (neoclassical felt piano) can serve as `seal` if Frank prefers.
- **Rights record.** Keep one line per bed: date, tool, plan, the prompt, and whether it was registered anywhere.
- **Buyer licence.** Personal listening; no redistribution.

### 4.3 The hub and the connectors

1. **Local MCP server** (plugin, stdio).
   - Read tools: `status`, `due`, `brief`, `insights`, `validate`, `library`, `program_day`.
   - Write tools: `witness_append`, `day_log`. They ask first, and are marked not read-only and not destructive.
   - Free.
2. **Public remote connector** (`/api/mcp` on Vercel).
   - Read-only tools: Library search, program days, loop prompts, `skill-check`.
   - No account, so no OAuth, and listable in the Claude directory quickly.
   - In ChatGPT it is a free practice surface only: OpenAI's rules forbid selling or linking to checkout.
3. **Personal hub connector** (after Studio Cloud). OAuth 2.0 through Better Auth.
   - Studio Cloud is end-to-end encrypted, so the server cannot read a person's practice. The personal connector
     therefore sees only an **agent view**: the fields the person chooses to share in plaintext, revocable at any time.
     Everything else stays sealed.
   - We state this trade-off; we never quietly weaken the encryption.
4. **MCP Apps.**
   - A Reality Card widget and a today's-practice widget, one `ui://` app that renders in Claude and ChatGPT.
   - The directory needs 3–5 screenshots at least 1000 px wide.

## 5. Money

### 5.1 The Complete Edition

- **Fees.**
  - Polar's fee depends on when the organization was created. Organizations from before 27 May 2026 keep 4% + 40¢
    unless they upgrade; newer ones on Starter pay 5% + 50¢.
  - Checked 2026-10-05: the only organization (`arcanea`) dates from 2026-07-13, and a new Reality Architect
    organization will be newer still, so **5% + 50¢** applies. `OFFER-AND-DELIVERY.md`'s 4% + 40¢ figures are
    therefore about 1 point optimistic.
  - Non-US cards add 1.5%.
- **Unit economics at $39:**
  - Starter: 5% + 50¢ = $2.45, so the margin is **94%**.
  - With a non-US card: $3.04, **92%**.
  - With the launch code ($29): $1.95, **93%**.
  - Serving each buyer costs us about nothing, because Polar stores and delivers the files.
- **One-time costs:**
  - narration $11–22;
  - music $0 (Frank's own);
  - tools $0;
  - covers from existing OpenArt credits.
- **Planning scenario, not a forecast.**
  - Visits to the program: 1,500–3,000 in the first two weeks, from Frank's channels.
  - Conversion: 1–3% buy.
  - That gives 15–90 sales, about **$0.5k–3.3k net**. Treat it as a range to measure against, not a promise.
- **Kill or continue.**
  - Fewer than 10 sales in the 14 days after launch with at least 1,500 visits: change the offer before changing the
    price.
  - Refunds above 10%: fix the product before any more promotion.

## 7. Innovation bets

1. **One engine, every surface.**
   - Where: the Studio, Claude Code, MCP, and later Obsidian mobile, all computing the same counts.
   - Already true: the Studio and the CLI are parity-tested.
   - Why it is hard to copy: rivals have an app *or* a format; we have the format, the engine, and honest numbers in
     every place the person already works.
2. **Rehearsal in your own words.**
   - The person's scene and "I am" lines become their own guided track.
   - It is rehearsal and attention practice, stated as such.
3. **Reality across time.** Sealed snapshots become a visual diff of the Atlas and a monthly report the person
   approves.
4. **The calendar as the bridge.**
   - Reps and bold moves go out as calendar events, and the witness loop asks whether each one happened.
   - That closes the loop between intent and evidence.
5. **MCP Apps.** A Reality Card and the day's practice inside Claude and ChatGPT, with no install.
6. **Guides.** Coaches run the practice with consented, revocable views. It scales the practice without scaling Frank.
7. **Spatial boards (Q1 2027).** A WebXR vision board for headsets. It is explored only after the core retains.

## 8. Risks and guardrails

- **Claims.**
  - The claims gate now scans `programs/`.
  - Tests forbid teacher names and causal or outcome words in the program.
  - Audio scripts and the book get the same review before release.
- **Voice.**
  - Frank's own voice only, with ElevenLabs' verification.
  - The synthetic voice is disclosed on every track and on the page.
- **Music.** The rights record per bed; Suno Pro or Premier at creation; no Content ID registration.
- **Payments.** Polar is the merchant of record; the refund is 30 days with no questions; each dispute costs $15.
- **Privacy.**
  - No account is needed for the edition.
  - No content analytics; Polar holds the sales numbers.
- **Disk and memory.** C: has 42 GiB free, under the 50 GiB rule, and RAM is under 2 GiB. So:
  - no installs or builds locally;
  - audio and EPUB builds are small ffmpeg and Pandoc runs;
  - CI builds the site.
