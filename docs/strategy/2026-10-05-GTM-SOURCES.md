## Voice and music

- ElevenLabs pricing: https://elevenlabs.io/pricing
  - Creator: $22 a month ($11 the first month), 121k credits.
  - Pro: $99 a month, 600k credits.
  - Commercial licence on every paid plan.
- ElevenLabs API pricing: https://elevenlabs.io/pricing/api
  - v3 and Multilingual v2: $0.08 per 1,000 characters.
  - Flash: $0.04 per 1,000 characters.
  - Music: $0.15 a minute.
- Models and credits: https://elevenlabs.io/docs/help-center/technical/what-models-do-you-offer-and-what-is-the-difference-between-them
- Professional Voice Cloning: https://elevenlabs.io/docs/eleven-creative/voices/voice-cloning/professional-voice-cloning
  - Available on Creator and higher.
  - Needs at least 30 minutes of audio.
  - Only your own voice may be cloned, and the voice must be verified.
- Commercial use of output: https://elevenlabs.io/docs/help-center/legal/can-i-publish-the-content-i-generate-on-the-platform
  - Output made on a paid plan stays commercial for good.
  - Beta services and free-plan output are excluded.
