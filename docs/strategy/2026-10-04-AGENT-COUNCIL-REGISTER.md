## Verdict

**Split at the block level, then ship.** Each artifact may hold both registers only as separate, labeled blocks:

- Library entries: Keep (practice) · Mechanism (evidence) · Limits (belief held lightly or not repeated).
- Studio fields: *desired* (the scene), *reported* (the present fact), *planned* (the act), *meaning* (what a sign
  meant to the person), each labeled in the UI and in every export.
- Copy never blends them into one causal sentence ("feel it and it will come").

Enforcement: `AGENTS.md` (Tone), `TASTE.md`, `scripts/check-public-claims.mjs`, and a Library test requiring Limits on
every entry.
