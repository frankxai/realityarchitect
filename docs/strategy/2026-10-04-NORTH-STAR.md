## 1. The decision in one paragraph

Reality Architect becomes **one coherent practice for architecting a life**: imagine the life you want, build the
systems and habits that carry you there, and witness the evidence as it arrives. It keeps the method's spine, the One
Law (*attention → belief → action → environment → feedback → outcome*), and adds the inner work Frank asked for:
imagination, identity, meaning, signs, and the full manifestation canon, taught **honestly**. Every teaching carries
two labels, **Meaning** (a contemplative lens, honored, not proven) and **Mechanism** (the studied pathway), plus its
**Limits**. The product is one brand on one domain (realityarchitect.ai), built on an **open file format**
(`reality.md` + `soul.md` + `reality/`). It has three surfaces: **Reality Studio** on the web, the **Reality Architect
agent plugin** for Claude Code and other harnesses, and **your own notes app** (Obsidian, including mobile) as a free
viewer. Starlight is the engine underneath, not a second consumer brand.

> **Imagine it. Build it. Witness it.**

## 3. Reality Theory: the three layers

The public frame that holds the whole practice together, and places every teacher on the map:

| Layer | What it is | What moves it | Practice in the product |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Perceived reality** | What reaches you. Perception is a model the brain builds from priors and attention (predictive processing). | Attention, expectation, what you have trained yourself to notice. | **Witness** ledger, the morning "look for" intention, primed vs unprimed signs. |
| **Inner reality** | Who is acting: identity, state, meaning, the scene you hold. | Imagination, rehearsal, self-talk, state, values. | **Soul** (soul.md), **Threshold** (the Imaginal Act), the "I am" lines. |
| **Outer reality** | What exists: systems, habits, environments, relationships, results. | Action, repetition, environment design, other people. | **Atlas** (12 domains), **Bridges** (skills, AI workflows, reps, bold moves, people, places), the Architect's Loop. |

Feedback ties the layers together: **Snapshots** (approved, dated) and **Decisions** show reality changing across time.
The honest claim, made everywhere: changes in perceived and inner reality are real, and they change outer reality
**through what you then do**. We never claim that thought alters the outer layer directly.

### The ten principles of Reality Architecture

The June 2026 brand brief's first next action ("define ten Reality Architecture principles"), written for the Library
and the agent plugin:

1. **Reality has three layers you can work on:** what you perceive, who you are being, and what exists.
2. **Outcomes travel one chain:** attention → belief → action → environment → feedback → outcome. Nothing skips it.
3. **Begin with the end as a scene,** a moment you could live in on an ordinary day, not a wish you chase.
4. **Face what is true today.** The obstacle belongs inside the plan, with an if-then response.
5. **Identity before outcome.** Act as the person who already lives there, one small vote at a time.
6. **Build the bridge:** skills to grow, systems to build, reps to repeat, bold moves to make, people and places to reach.
7. **Let systems carry what willpower cannot:** environments, defaults, and agents.
8. **Witness honestly:** record what happened, what it meant to you, and what you did, misses included.
9. **Seal the moment.** Approve snapshots so you can see reality change across time.
10. **Your words, your data, your agency.** You own the file; agents propose and never impose; other people keep their
    own will.

### Starlight Second Brain vs Obsidian: the recommendation

**Do not build an Obsidian competitor. Make Reality Architect the visual practice layer that works with any vault.**

- Obsidian already has mobile, sync, plugins and a decade of polish. A second editor would eat a year and still lose.
- Our edge is the **practice**: protocols, curriculum, agents, and visuals of a life changing over time. Editing notes
  is not the edge.
- Plain files make us complementary. `reality/` is visible in Obsidian (dot-folders are hidden there, which is why
  v0.2 adds "vault mode"). JSON Canvas is the open format Obsidian created, so the Reality Map opens on a phone today.
- **Starlight** stays the engine and Frank's reference instance: SIS indexes the vault, `second-brain-os` ingests chats,
  agents read and propose through SIP. It is not marketed as a separate notes app.
- Later, an OSS **Obsidian plugin** renders Bridges, Witness and Timeline inside Obsidian, so the full experience
  reaches mobile without our own native app.

## 6. The practice (what a person does)

1. **Imagine** (Threshold, 8 minutes): author one scene from a chosen domain, face one present fact and one obstacle,
   choose one act with a date and a proof. Leaves with a Reality Card.
2. **Map** (Atlas, 20 minutes): rate twelve life domains for *now* and *wanted*, write one true sentence and one scene
   per domain that matters, and pick at most three priorities. Coverage, not twelve simultaneous projects.
3. **Bridge** (per priority): the path from now to the scene. Human skills to grow, AI workflows to build (the five-move
   Loop), reps with a weekly cadence, bold moves with dates, people to meet, environments to enter. The obstacle gets an
   if-then plan (mental contrasting with implementation intentions). An inspectable **"Is it enough?"** check compares
   planned reps against logged reps and dated moves, and gives a plain answer.
4. **Daily** (Today, 2 to 10 minutes): morning: read the "I am" lines, rest with the scene for a minute, set one thing
   to look for and one rep. Evening: witness what happened, what was noticed, what was done, and one correction. A
   one-line path exists for tired days. No streaks, no punishment.
5. **Witness** (any time): log signs, wins, openings, reps, moves, lessons and gratitude. Each entry separates **what
   happened** (fact) from **what it meant** (meaning, the person's own) and **what I did** (action). Signs you set out
   to notice are counted against signs that arrived unprompted: E² done as a real experiment, with misses counted too.
6. **Seal** (weekly or monthly): the Studio drafts a snapshot (domain scores, bridge pace, evidence counts, a
   reflection); the person approves it and it becomes immutable. Diffs between snapshots show reality across time.
7. **Decide**: life and business decisions as dated records with options, choice, reasons, and a review date, so the
   person can later see which decisions shaped which results.
8. **Learn** (Library): Reality Theory, the honest canon, and reading paths.

## 7. The Library: Reality Theory and the honest canon

Public, original synthesis in our own words, no copied book text. Three shelves:

- **Meaning shelf**, the manifestation teachers: Neville Goddard, Joe Dispenza, Marisa Peer, Tony Robbins, Rhonda Byrne
  (*The Secret*), Pam Grout (*E-Squared*), Joseph Murphy, Esther and Jerry Hicks, Wayne Dyer, James Allen, Napoleon
  Hill, Maxwell Maltz. Each gets **Keep** (the practice worth doing), **Mechanism** (the studied pathway it rides on),
  **Limits** (the claims we do not repeat), and **In the Studio** (where the practice lives).
- **Mechanism shelf**, the science that makes the practices work: Oettingen (mental contrasting), Gollwitzer
  (implementation intentions), Bandura (self-efficacy), Clear and Fogg (habits), Seth and Feldman Barrett (constructed
  perception and emotion), Emmons and Seligman (gratitude, three good things), Wiseman (noticing chance
  opportunities), Lally (habit timelines), meditation meta-analyses, the Stoics.
- **Frontier shelf**, quantum and philosophy, labeled as philosophy: Wheeler (participatory universe), Bohm (implicate
  order), Rovelli (relational quantum mechanics), QBism (Fuchs), Hoffman (interface theory), Penrose-Hameroff (Orch-OR),
  Jung (synchronicity as meaningful, acausal coincidence). The rule is stated plainly: none of these establishes that a
  wish changes an external event, and the product never says it does.

The claims gate (`scripts/check-public-claims.mjs`) keeps working: teacher names may appear only in the Library data
file, where a test enforces that every entry carries Limits, and new patterns block the risky phrases ("quantum
proof", "raise your vibration", "attract money", guaranteed results, disease-healing claims).

## 9. Doctrine changes (this PR)

The current tone contract says "No spiritual or hype language", which Codex's reviewer correctly enforced against
#38. Frank's 2026-10-04 directive changes the doctrine:

- **Two registers are allowed, labeled, never blended.** Meaning-register language (spiritual lens, imagination,
  assumption, signs, synchronicity, gratitude) is welcome when it is labeled as a contemplative perspective or as the
  person's own meaning. Mechanism-register language carries every causal claim.
- **Still forbidden:** claims that thought, feeling, energy, frequency or quantum effects change external events
  directly; health, wealth, income or relationship promises; blaming people for their circumstances; hype.
- **The One Law stays the discriminator** for anything presented as cause and effect.

`AGENTS.md`, `TASTE.md`, `SYSTEM.md` and `DESIGN.md` are updated to match.

## 12. Guardrails and ethics (non-negotiable)

1. No claim that thought, feeling, frequency, "vibration" or quantum effects change external events directly.
2. No health, wealth, income, or relationship outcome promises. Meditation and imagery are never presented as medical
   treatment.
3. Never blame a person for their circumstances. A missing result is information about the plan, not a verdict on
   their consciousness.
4. Signs are recorded honestly: the meaning is the person's; the system does not certify causation.
5. Local-first by default; every sync, AI use, or share is explicit, revocable consent.
6. Crisis language routes to human help (local emergency services, crisis lines); agents do not counsel through a
   crisis.
7. Other people keep their own agency: no practice targets a specific person's choices.

## 15a. Guarding the focus (the toolsmith trap)

Frank's own reflection notes warn against two failure modes: building tools instead of closing paying work, and
starting new repos or brands that become the next thing to consolidate. This plan answers both on purpose:

- **No new repo, no new brand.** Everything lands in `realityarchitect`. The Lightbook / Agentic Lifebook / Soulbook
  chain ends here: its life-domain diagnostic becomes the Studio's Atlas, a feature, not a new spine.
- **Connection, not construction.** Obsidian stays the editor and the phone app, Starlight stays the engine, and
  second-brain-os stays the chat pipeline. We wrote a file format and a practice layer, not another notes app.
- **The commercial job of this work is to serve the current paid wedge.** The Studio's Atlas plus `soul.md` is the
  onboarding diagnostic for Frank's existing client offer. No new paid Reality Architect tier (Vault, Diff pack,
  Studio Cloud, Academy) opens until that wedge is closed; until then, the 30-day metrics in §14 are the only goal.
- **Next 30 days are evidence, not features:** Frank uses the Studio and the plugin himself every day, one client
  onboarding uses the Atlas, and the first five strangers' Threshold cards (with consent) are read for what to fix.
