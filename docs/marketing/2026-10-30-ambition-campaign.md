# Reality Architect — Your ambition deserves a system

Campaign direction · 30 October 2026 · Europe/Amsterdam

Owner brief: more confident, proud, ambitious marketing, with a clear offer and usable product. This direction applies that brief to the existing Reality Architect product. Built on SIP.

## The decision

Lead with **Build what you keep imagining.** Keep **Imagine it. Build it. Witness it.** as the enduring method signature. Use **Your ambition deserves a system.** as the campaign's identity statement.

The first conversion is a completed Reality Card. The visitor chooses a scene, names a present fact and obstacle, plans an act, and exports their own words. Studio and the thirty-day practice give that first artifact somewhere to develop.

Initial campaign audience: independent creators and founders with one project they keep postponing. The broader brand can serve a whole life; each advertisement should name one recognizable situation.

## Offer that can be promoted from current accepted source

**Reality Card — free.** A scene worth building, a fact to face, and one next move you control.

Primary destination: https://www.realityarchitect.ai/threshold

Primary CTA: **Create my Reality Card**

Supporting line: **Free. No account. Download a card you own.**

The first useful result is the user's authored card. The larger ambition gives the experience emotional meaning; the invitation makes no claim that a specific external outcome will follow.

## Proof before scale

Run a twelve-person activation pilot: six relevant people from the founder's audience and six people unfamiliar with the brand. This is a usability and offer-understanding study, not statistical proof of conversion uplift.

Ask each person to use the page, create a card, and decide whether to return. Do not request their private scene or journal. With permission, record completion, export, help required, and whether they took their chosen act by their own date. User reports remain labeled self-reports.

Internal decision criteria: nine of twelve explain the offer correctly; eight export without assistance; six report taking their chosen act within the observation window. These thresholds are operating choices, not research-derived benchmarks. Inspect every failed session and revise the experience before purchasing traffic. Report counts, sample selection, observation window and missing responses.

No new tracking is enabled here. The accepted product has no analytics provider. A future consent-respecting measurement design may record coarse events such as `card_started`, `card_exported`, and `practice_returned`; it must never include scene, obstacle, personal notes, or card content. Campaign labels alone do not establish attribution.

## The role of the portfolio

FrankX carries the founder's voice and brings qualified attention. Reality Architect provides direction and practice. Starlight supplies agent infrastructure. GenCreator supports production. Arcanea carries creative worlds and IP. Those relationships support fulfillment; the first screen still offers one useful result.

For another portfolio product, reuse this contract: **one person, one desired result, one inspectable first output, one continuation, one evidence source.** Put repository depth behind proof and delivery. A buyer should be able to describe the product without counting repositories or agents.
