# Taste Contract

## Thesis

An architectural instrument, not a transformation manifesto.

## Keep

- The five dependent moves: See, Design, Build, Automate, Compound.
- Blueprint/graphite identity.
- Open method and forkable templates.
- Concrete, technical, understated language.

## Cut

- Unsupported superlatives and life/income promises.
- Claims that systems remove human responsibility.
- Private-vault mystique, unverified prices, scarcity, and outcome claims.
- Fake capture forms and conceptual proof presented as production evidence.

## Signature

Every visitor can identify one gap, name one next artifact, and export a Markdown architecture brief without creating an account or sending private context to a server.
