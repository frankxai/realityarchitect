# Taste Contract

## Thesis

An architectural instrument for a whole life, not a transformation manifesto. The inner work and the systems work sit
side by side, each in its own labeled register, under one law.

## Keep

- The five dependent moves: See, Design, Build, Automate, Compound.
- Blueprint/graphite identity for structure, numbers, and mechanism.
- The dawn register (warm `#e8d5ad`, Georgia) for meaning, and for the person's own words.
- Open method, open standard, forkable templates.
- Concrete, technical, understated language, and contemplative language that says what it is.

## Cut

- Unsupported superlatives and life, income, health, or relationship promises.
- Causal claims for thought, feeling, frequency, vibration, or quantum effects acting on external events.
- Claims that systems remove human responsibility, and readings that blame a person for their circumstances.
- Private-vault mystique, unverified prices, scarcity, streak shame, and outcome claims.
- Fake capture forms and conceptual proof presented as production evidence.

## Signature

Every visitor can identify one gap, author one scene, name one next act or artifact, and export it as Markdown, without
creating an account or sending private context to a server. Their own words appear in dawn; the system's in blueprint.
